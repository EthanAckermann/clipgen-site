CLIPGEN is a private tool run by one creator for their own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Use

CLIPGEN only sends videos to TikTok accounts that have signed in and given permission through TikTok's own login screen. Videos are sent to the account's TikTok drafts (inbox). Nothing is posted publicly until the account owner chooses to post it in the TikTok app.

Content

Clips are short moments from public live streams, edited and credited to the original streamer. If you are a streamer and want your content removed, contact us and it will be taken down.

TikTok

Use of TikTok through CLIPGEN is also covered by TikTok's own Terms of Service. You can remove CLIPGEN's access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions.
