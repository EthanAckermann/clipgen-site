Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What we collect

When the account owner connects TikTok, CLIPGEN receives from TikTok: the account's display name, an account ID, and access tokens that allow it to upload videos to that account's drafts. That is all.

How it is used

This information is only used to upload the creator's own clips to their own TikTok drafts. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

It is stored only on the creator's own computer. It is not sent to any other server apart from TikTok.

Deleting your data

You can remove CLIPGEN's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens. You can also contact us to ask for deletion and it will be done straight away.
