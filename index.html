CLIPGEN

CLIPGEN is a personal tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @clipgenerational and the YouTube channel @clipgenerational.

It turns short moments from public Twitch streams into vertical clips with captions. When a clip is ready, CLIPGEN can send it to the creator's own TikTok drafts (inbox) using TikTok's Content Posting API. The creator then reviews, edits and posts it themselves in the TikTok app. CLIPGEN never posts on its own and is only used with the creator's own TikTok account.

Every clip credits the original streamer.
